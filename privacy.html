Nobei Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Nobei is a shopping calculator, price comparer and list app for Android, made by Musonda Himululi. This policy explains what information Nobei handles and why.

Information that stays on your phone

Your shopping lists, budgets, prices and settings (currency, language, theme and animations) are saved only on your phone. Nobei has no accounts and no servers of its own, and the developer never receives or sees this information. Uninstalling Nobei, or clearing its storage, deletes it.

Advertising

Nobei is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Nobei asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Nobei under Home > Settings > Privacy choices.

On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings under Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).

Children

Nobei is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
